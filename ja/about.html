会社情報 PinForge

Your trusted manufacturing partner for premium custom pins & badges

10+ Years of Crafting Excellence

Founded in 2015 in Dongguan — China's manufacturing capital — PinForge has grown from a small workshop to a modern manufacturing facility with 12 production lines and 200+ skilled artisans.

We specialize in custom enamel pins, button badges, acrylic standees, and metal keychains for B2B clients worldwide. Our mission: make premium-quality custom pins accessible to brands of all sizes, with low MOQs and factory-direct pricing.

Today, we serve 500+ active clients across 30+ countries, including brand merchandise managers, anime/IP licensing companies, event organizers, and promotional product distributors.
