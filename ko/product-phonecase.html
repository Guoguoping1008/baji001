Printing & Finish Options

6 professional techniques to make your design stand out

UV Printing

Direct UV print on case surface. Vibrant colors, scratch-resistant. Best for detailed designs.

Sublimation

Heat-transfer dye process. Edge-to-edge printing. Ideal for photo-realistic images.

Embossing

Raised 3D logo effect. Premium tactile feel. Perfect for luxury brands.

Silkscreen

Classic single/multi-color print. Cost-effective for solid color logos.

Hot Stamping

Gold/silver/rose gold foil logos. Luxury accent. Great for corporate gifts.

IMD (In-Mold Decoration)

Print embedded under protective layer. Most durable. Premium long-lasting finish.

FAQ

Common questions from B2B buyers

What's the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

50 units minimum, with mixed iPhone models allowed. For custom molds (new iPhone models not yet in our catalog), MOQ is 200-500 units. The more you order, the better the unit price.

Can I get samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sample cost is typically 3-5× unit price, refundable upon bulk order of 1,000+ pcs. Sample lead time: 5-7 days. Mixed sample packs available (3-5 styles × 1-2 units each).

What file format do you need for artwork?

Adobe Illustrator (.ai), Photoshop (.psd), PDF (vector preferred), or high-resolution PNG/JPG (300dpi minimum). Our design team can also create artwork from sketches or reference images — included free.

Do you offer retail packaging?

Yes. Options include: (1) Standard plastic bag (free), (2) Custom printed OPP bag ($0.08/pc), (3) Premium gift box ($0.85-2.50/pc), (4) Eco-friendly kraft packaging ($0.45/pc). Custom designs available for 1,000+ unit orders.

Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We provide FNSKU labeling, poly bagging, bubble wrap, and box labeling per Amazon's requirements. DDP service to US FBA warehouses available with all duties paid.

What's your warranty / return policy?

12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Defect rate guaranteed below 1%. We replace defective units at no cost. Quality inspection report provided before each shipment.
