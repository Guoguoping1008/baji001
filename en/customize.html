Frequently Asked Questions

720|711|

Common questions from our B2B clients

721|712|
722|713|
723|714|
724|715| What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)? 725|716|

Our MOQ starts at just 50 pieces for most products. For complex custom designs requiring new molds, MOQ may be 100-200 pieces. We're one of the few factories willing to accept small-batch orders.

726|717|
727|718|
728|719| How long does production take? 729|720|

Standard production: 7-10 business days after artwork approval. Rush service: 3-5 business days (additional 30% fee). Shipping time varies by destination — typically 3-7 days by DHL/FedEx express.

730|721|
731|722|
732|723| Do you provide samples before mass production? 733|724|

Yes. Sample cost is typically 3x unit price, refundable upon bulk order of 500+ pcs. Sample lead time: 5-7 days. You can also order samples of existing catalog items at MOQ pricing.

734|725|
735|726|
736|727| What payment methods do you accept? 737|728|

We accept T/T (bank wire), PayPal (for samples/small orders), Western Union, and L/C for large orders. New clients: 50% deposit + 50% before shipping. Established clients: 30/70 terms available.

738|729|
739|730|
740|731| Do you ship worldwide? What about customs/duties? 741|732|

Yes, we ship to 30+ countries. We offer DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) service to US/EU/UK/CA/AU — we handle all customs and duties, you receive the package hassle-free. Standard shipping: EXW or FOB.

742|733|
743|734|
744|735| Can you help with design? 745|736|

Absolutely! Our in-house design team provides free mockups and unlimited revisions. Just send us a sketch, reference image, or even just an idea — we'll turn it into production-ready artwork.

746|737|
747|738|
748|739| What file formats do you accept? 749|740|

Adobe Illustrator (.ai), Photoshop (.psd), PDF (vector preferred), CorelDRAW (.cdr), or high-resolution PNG/JPG (300dpi minimum). Don't have a design? Our team can create one from scratch.

750|741|
751|742|
752|743| How do you ensure quality? 753|744|

Three-stage QC: (1) Pre-production sample approval, (2) In-line inspection during production, (3) Final AQL 2.5 inspection before packing. We share photos/videos of finished goods before shipment.
