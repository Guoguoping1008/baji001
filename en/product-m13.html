M13 FAQ

Common questions from B2B buyers

Which details should I provide for a quotation?

Please provide quantity, artwork, packaging, destination market, required documents and your preferred delivery date. Final specifications are confirmed before ordering.

Can I customize the app with my own branding?

Yes — white-label app service is available for orders of 1,000+ units. We provide the app shell, your logo, color scheme, and content library. App store submission support included.

What's the battery life in real use?

6-8 hours of active screen-on use. With power-saving mode (sleep when not in use), battery lasts 24+ hours. Type-C charging takes ~90 minutes for full charge.

Is there a monthly app subscription fee?

No. The companion app is free for end-users. We only charge a one-time SDK integration fee for B2B white-label clients.

Can users upload their own images?

Yes! End users can upload any JPG/PNG/GIF (≤10s video) via the app. Content is stored locally on device + cloud backup.

What's included in the box?

M13 device, magnetic clip, Type-C charging cable, user manual, optional retail packaging. Custom packaging available for 500+ unit orders.
